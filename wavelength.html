How the
score works

Four comparisons, weighted by how much they predict a friendship that sticks. Artists count most because they're the most specific.

Each category is compared with cosine similarity on sets, so someone who lists three artists isn't punished next to someone who lists ten. The weighted total is passed through a gentle curve so partial overlap still reads as a real signal.

// for each category k
sim(k) = |A ∩ B| / √(|A| · |B|)

// weighted blend
raw = 0.40·artists + 0.25·genres
    + 0.25·interests + 0.10·goals

// curve to a 0–100 score
score = 100 · (1 − (1 − raw)2.2)
